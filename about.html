About Us

We’ve been doing this work for a long time. We’re here to do it better.

Most consultants want to expand your vision because they want to expand their billing. Our first question is always the opposite: what if we did less? What if we didn’t do anything at all?

We’re not interested in work for work’s sake. If you give us a dollar, we need to give you back ten. That’s the standard we hold ourselves to — and it’s why we started Magic Button Labs.

How we work

The value was never in the clicking.

The consulting industry charged by the hour because hours were the only way to measure work that happened at keyboards. We work through Claude — which means the execution is fast and the cost of repetitive tasks has collapsed. What hasn’t changed is the part that matters.

01

You pay for our thinking

We’re with you on calls, asking the right questions and translating what we hear into decisions. We design the architecture, identify the edge cases, and decide which changes are worth making at all. That judgment is unchanged by AI — it’s still human, still ours, and still the most expensive thing to get right.

02

Claude executes. We direct.

Once we know what to build, Claude builds it. What used to take an afternoon of careful configuration takes minutes — and the system stays coherent because we’re managing the machine, not just issuing instructions to it. The efficiency flows back to you in the form of more thinking per dollar.

03

Every engagement improves the next

Our methodology lives in structured files that Claude reads every session. Each client teaches us something that sharpens the standard for every client that follows. You don’t just get a monday.com build — you get the accumulated judgment of every build we’ve done, applied to yours.
