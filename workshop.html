How it works

Learn it. See it. Build it.

Every session follows the same structure: we teach the concept, show you what it looks like in a real system, then put you inside a pre-built lab environment with real boards and exercises designed to make the lesson stick.

Sessions are delivered live and designed to fit around active implementation. Most clients spread the series across two to four weeks. You don’t have to do it all at once — and the labs are self-contained so the order can flex to what your team needs first.

The curriculum

Eight sessions. Each one changes how you build.

From foundational board structure to team adoption — the complete blueprint for building monday.com the right way.

01

Boards & Views

Structure boards so work is visible without being cluttered. Learn the mental models serious builders use from day one.

02

Dashboards

Build dashboards leadership actually reads. Connect your data so the right numbers surface automatically.

03

Relational Data

Connect boards so your data flows instead of duplicates. Stop copy-pasting between boards and start building a real source of truth.

04

Automations

Make work move forward without someone pushing it. Build automation logic that holds up as your system grows.

Design alerts people actually respond to. Stop the noise — surface the signals that matter at the moment they matter.

06

Control who sees what and who can do what — without creating friction for the people who need to get work done.

07

Evaluate your system before it becomes a mess. Learn how to make structural decisions that hold up at scale.

08

Team Adoption

Launch monday.com so your team actually uses it. Build the rollout plan, onboarding docs, and working agreements that make it stick.
