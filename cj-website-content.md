## i. Sales & Growth

**Section intro:**
Every lead caught, every opportunity tracked, every deal closed with the right product on the right terms — and the whole business ready before the ink dries.

### Modules

**🌱 Lead Capture and Qualification**
Every lead caught, every conversation tracked, nothing slips through. When someone's ready to buy, you know.

**🌳 Sales Opportunity Management**
Run your pipeline, forecast revenue, and hand off to delivery the moment a deal is real — not weeks later when paperwork catches up.

**📦 Products and Services Catalogue**
Define what you sell once. Sales picks from the menu, delivery knows what was promised, and the team stops arguing over scope.

---

## ii. Won to Paid

**Section intro:**
From signature to payment to commission. The handoff, the invoice, the payout — handled, without the usual chaos in between.

### Modules

**💰 Financial Operations and Invoicing**
Upload the contract. AI builds the invoice schedule. Accounting takes it from there. The endless back-and-forth between sales and accounting just stops.

**💸 Sales Commissions**
When the invoice gets paid, the commission gets calculated. Splits, payees, payment history — handled, with visibility limited to the people who need it.

**🤝 Post-Sales Handoff**
The delivery team starts prepping the moment a deal is real. No more "we just signed, can you staff this Monday?" panics.

---

## iii. Delivery & Operations

**Section intro:**
Where the work actually happens. Project health, hours burned, capacity, and a place where every client collaborates with you — all connected, all visible.

### Modules

**💬 Project Management**
Every active project on one screen. Health, deadlines, what's left to do, and what needs your attention today.

**⏱️ Time Tracking and Profitability**
Know which projects are profitable and which are bleeding hours — weeks before it becomes a crisis.

**⚖️ Resource Planning and Capacity**
See who's actually overbooked, including the projects that haven't closed yet. Staff your next deal before you sign it.

**🛠️ Project Execution and Guest Collaboration**
Every client gets their own collaboration space. They see their project, nothing else. No more email chains and shared spreadsheets.
