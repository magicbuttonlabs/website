## Source material (webinar transcript)
- **Living vs. shelf strategy** (Anti-Patterns): "strategic planning and execution should be a living thing that is ever ongoing and visible… not an exercise that you do and then sits on the shelf and gets dusty and then gets picked up off the shelf and dusted off and then put back on the shelf." (Ethan)
- **Visibility as first principle:** "make everything visible and know your baselines"; "you can't measure things you can't see. So visibility is the first principle… If you can't see what we're about to show you, good luck fixing it." L'Amour: "before you can control conditions, you must first recognize them." (cited by Ethan)
- **The universal planning problem** (Same Call, Different Company): "we'd often see this kind of universal planning problem… constantly replanning and never quite sure… confusion of which team owns it, at what level, at what detail, and what needs to be actually delivered… I'm done with that, but it's done to one team, but not management… you have no idea how it's going." Seen "across multiple industries… enterprise agile transformations to… manufacturing." (Brandon & Ethan)
- **AI risk capture** (Why Didn't Anyone Tell Me?!): automation — "anytime an update is created, then use AI [to] look in the updates section for any references to potential risks and update the risks field with all risks from all updates"; "I don't need to think about the risk field. It's just getting captured there." Plus AI building a risk-register doc template on request, saved as a reusable default. (Ethan demo)
- **Adjacent material not yet used** (future Strategy spokes/pillar): the goals→objectives→initiatives→projects hierarchy; leading vs. lagging indicators; centralized strategy / decentralized execution; the four universal goals; now/next/next+1/maybe; Monday Dev (hierarchy) + Work Management (execution) "bridge."
