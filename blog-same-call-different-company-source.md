## Entry 1 — "Every company switching to monday.com is afraid of the same thing"

**Status:** Published 2026-06-02 · **Lane:** Pattern · **Blog post:** `blog-monday-migration-fear.html` · **Byline:** Brandon Huff

### The pattern
Teams migrating to monday.com from very different tools (HubSpot, Salesforce, Asana, Wrike, Basecamp, spreadsheets) arrive with the same two fears: the migration will be a painful slog, and the team won't adopt the new tool. The empathy beat: you're not the only one — and the migration fear is usually aimed at the wrong thing.

### Synthesis
Empathy + reframe. Normalize the fear (it's universal), then reframe it: the dreaded part (migrating configurations) is the part you shouldn't do — you're leaving the old tool because it was set up badly. Move the data that matters onto a clean architecture; the move is often DIY-simple. Adoption is a real but separate, solvable worry. Close: "everything's going to be okay" — earned.

### Frequency framing
Qualitative only ("nearly every migration call," "company after company," "I've talked to a lot of companies like that"). No invented count.
