## Entry 1 — "Wait — can everyone in my account see this board?"

**Status:** Published 2026-06-02 · **Lane:** Pattern · **Blog post:** `blog-monday-board-privacy.html` · **Byline:** Ethan Foulkes

### The aha
Clients panic that anyone with a login can see boards holding sensitive data (salaries, SSNs, HR). The reassurance: private boards are invisible even to admins. The kicker nobody is told: hiding a column is clarity, not security — it can be duplicated and unhidden.

### Synthesis
Reassure (private = invisible, even to admins; permission is the board's, not your role) → one wrinkle (names visible in the content directory, contents not) → shareable for external guests → **the kicker** (hiding ≠ securing; duplicate-and-unhide) → finer control (item-level visibility) → the point (set permissions deliberately at board level; don't rely on hiding).
