How We Deliver

Five phases. Zero surprises.

When you work with us, you purchase a bucket of hours — and our delivery team runs a proven five-phase process to turn those hours into results. Every engagement follows the same path, so you always know where your hours are going, what’s coming next, and what it would take to go further.

The five phases

How your hours become outcomes.

Every phase has a goal and an output you can see — and nothing moves forward until you’ve confirmed it.

01

Analyze

We learn how your business runs, where it hurts, and everything monday.com could do about it — then aim your hours at the highest-ROI problems first. We also align on how much your team wants to own versus have us deliver. This is the most important phase: if the appetite is bigger than the budget, you find out here — not at the end.

Goal

A shared, prioritized view of your needs — sized honestly against your hours.

Output

A prioritized backlog of deliverables, mapped to your budget. It has value on its own — whether we build it, your team does, or someone else does.

02

Prototype

We reflect what we heard in a basic working build — starting with simple diagrams of how things relate, then real boards with your data — so you can confirm the direction before we invest deeper.

Goal

Reflect your needs in a basic build that’s directionally right.

Output

An initial build you’ve seen and confirmed matches your understanding and needs.

03

Iterate

With the direction confirmed, we build out the nuanced pieces that get it over the line — views, dashboards, refinements — checking off backlog goals as your team tests in the system.

Goal

Continue the build against the backlog until the focused scope is done.

Output

The solution, demoed and approved — with your team testing inside it.

04

Optimize

Optional

If the core is approved and hours remain, we lean into what extends it most — a basic integration, an advanced dashboard, more views per role. If the budget’s done, we skip straight to Handoff. No padding.

Goal

Extend an approved solution with the enhancements that matter most.

Output

Each enhancement demoed and approved — or a deliberate decision to skip this phase.

05

Handoff

We formally package the solution and close the gaps that help your team run it — recordings, walkthroughs, final touches. Minimal hours, by design. There should be no surprises left by the time we get here.

Goal

Your team confidently owning the solution.

Output

A packaged, documented system — owned by you.

What comes after
Closed — and clear on what’s next.

Every engagement closes completely — the work accepted, the solution owned by your team. What remains is the backlog: the prioritized list of everything else your system could do. Tackle it in-house, or bring us back — more hours, a retainer, a new team, the next phase. Either way, you know exactly what’s next.
