Why it works

Built to make every hour count.

The five phases

How your journey plays out.

Every phase has a goal and a deliverable you can see — and nothing moves forward until you’ve confirmed it.

01

Analyze

We learn how your business runs, where it hurts, and everything monday.com could do about it — then aim our expertise at the highest-ROI problems first. We also align on how much your team wants to own versus have us deliver. This is the most important phase: expectations get set here, so there are no surprises later.

Goal

A shared, prioritized view of your needs — and where to focus first.

What you get

A prioritized backlog of deliverables, mapped to your budget. It has value on its own — whether we build it, your team does, or someone else does.

02

Prototype

We reflect what we heard in a basic working build — starting with simple diagrams of how things relate, then real boards with your data — so you can confirm the direction before we invest deeper.

Goal

Reflect your needs in a basic build that’s directionally right.

What you get

An initial build you’ve seen and confirmed matches your understanding and needs.

03

Iterate

With the direction confirmed, we build out the nuanced pieces that get it over the line — views, dashboards, refinements — checking off backlog goals as your team tests in the system.

Goal

Continue the build against the backlog until the focused scope is done.

What you get

The solution, demoed and approved — with your team testing inside it.

04

Handoff

We formally package the solution and close the gaps that help your team run it — recordings, walkthroughs, final touches. There should be no surprises left by the time we get here.

Goal

Your team confidently owning the solution.

What you get

A packaged, documented system — owned by you.

05

Support & Optimize

Optional

You didn’t buy a static, off-the-shelf tool — you bought a flexible platform that keeps doing more as your business grows. So the only question after handoff is who does the optimizing: your team, now that you own it, or ours. Stay on with support, lean into enhancements, add new teams, training, or new builds — or take it from here. Either way, we’ve got you covered.

Goal

A solution that keeps growing with your business — with us or on your own.

What you get

Support, optimization, training, and new builds when you want them. Entirely your call.

Ready to supercharge your business?

Five proven phases, decades of expertise — pointed wherever your business needs them. New teams, training, builds, support: we’ve got you covered.
