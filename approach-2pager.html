How we keep you in control

What you can count on

  • Your needs mapped to the five phases at delivery kickoff.
  • A backlog with standalone value from phase one.
  • A flag the moment an idea outgrows the engagement — not at the end.

What we won't do

  • Drown you in analysis theater. As little analysis as possible — as much as we need.
  • Quietly build past what you signed up for. If something needs more, we talk about it when it comes up.
  • Hand off a system your team can't run.
